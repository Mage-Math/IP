Page 1: The Everyday Idea

Painting a Fence 🎨

Teacher's Hint: Inverse proportion means: "When one quantity goes UP, the other goes DOWN at the exact same rate!"
1 painter takes 12 hours to paint a fence.
2 painters take 6 hours to paint the same fence!

If you hire 4 painters, what happens to the time needed?

Page 2: The Magic Total

Finding the Constant Total (k) 🔑

In inverse proportion, multiplying the two numbers together always gives the exact same result (k)!
Building a Wall:
1 worker × 30 days = 30 worker-days
2 workers × 15 days = 30 worker-days

Let's practice: If 3 workers take 10 days, what is the constant total work required (k)?

3 workers × 10 days = total worker-days
Page 3: The Easy Formula

Writing the Formula 📝

To find the time or amount needed (y), divide the total work (k) by the number of helpers (x):
y = k ÷ x  or  y = k⁄x
(Time = Total Work ÷ Helpers)

If the total work is k = 24, how many hours will it take 6 workers?

y = 24 ÷ 6 = hours
Page 4: Real-World Example

Road Trip Speed 🚗

A car traveling at 60 mph takes 2 hours to reach a city.

Step 1: Find the total distance (k = speed × time):

60 × 2 = miles

Step 2: If you drive faster at 120 mph, how long will the trip take (y = k ÷ x)?

Time = 120 miles ÷ 120 mph = hour(s)
Page 5: The Curve

What does it look like on a Graph? 📈

2 Golden Rules of Inverse Proportion Graphs:
  1. It forms a downward curve (called a hyperbola).
  2. It never touches zero because having 0 helpers means the job is never finished!
